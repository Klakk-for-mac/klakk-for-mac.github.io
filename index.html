How It Works

1. Install and Launch

Drag Klakk to Applications. It appears in your menu bar. Already activated with full access to all features.

2. Select Your Sound Pack

Choose from 14 premium packs. Preview instantly. Pick the mechanical feel that matches your style.

3. Adjust and Enable

Fine-tune volume. Toggle on. Klakk monitors keystrokes system-wide with sub-10ms latency.

4. Type in Silence

Hear satisfying mechanical sounds through headphones or speakers. Your physical keyboard remains silent to others.
